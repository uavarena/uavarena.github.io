Autonomous UAVs, real autopilots, one arena.

UAV Arena is a virtual competition where every aircraft runs real ArduPlane autopilot software and is commanded over MAVLink, exactly as on a real airframe. An automatic referee scores every lock-on, dive and landing, live. Teams test their autonomy code against real opponents without risking an aircraft.

How a match works

  1. Take off

    Each team's aircraft takes off within the take-off window, flown by the team's own code or by a pilot with a ground station.

  2. Lock on

    Hold an opponent inside your camera's hit zone for 4 seconds. The referee checks every claim against ground truth.

  3. Dive for the QR code

    Dive at the ground pad and read its QR code to complete the dive task.

  4. Land

    Land in the landing zone before time runs out. Geofence breaches, no-fly zones and unsafe flying cost points.

Under the hood

  • Every aircraft is a real ArduPlane autopilot (ArduPilot SITL), commanded over MAVLink
  • An automatic referee validates lock-ons: camera projection, hit zone, hold time and gaps
  • Live penalties for geofence, no-fly zones, unsafe proximity, telemetry loss and false claims
  • 3D view in Gazebo, plus a web dashboard with scoreboard and referee console
  • Teams join from their own laptops over the network, or fly by hand with Mission Planner
  • Rules live in one configuration file, so formats can change without code changes

What test matches taught us

Deconfliction comes before tactics.Two aircraft running identical code collided 24 seconds after take-off.
Aim past the target.A guided dive failed on every pass until the aim point was moved beyond the QR pad; then every team read the code on its first dive.
Stagger the landings.Two aircraft returning at the same moment met on final approach.

Who it's for

Universities and leagues

Run courses, internal team selection or a full league on one platform, with automatic scoring and replays.

Companies

Test multi-aircraft autonomy software against standard scenarios before risking an airframe.

For companies

Testing several autonomous aircraft together on real hardware is slow, expensive and risky. UAV Arena runs the same autopilot software in a shared virtual airspace, with automatic scoring and full logs of every flight.

  • Test your autonomy code against standard multi-aircraft scenarios before a flight test
  • Run a hiring challenge for interns and graduates, under your own brand
  • Train new engineers on MAVLink, ArduPilot and multi-aircraft operations without hardware
  • Run it on your own network, with your aircraft models and closed scenarios (in development)
